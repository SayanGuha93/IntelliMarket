# Dashboard page plan

| Page | Proposed visuals and measures |
|---|---|
| Marketplace Overview | Product Count and Marketplace Share cards and bars. Category and brand ranking using Product Count, Category Share and Brand Share. Include the Unrecorded bucket and category basis. |
| Price and Discount Analytics | Price-band distribution, Price Coverage and Median Recorded Price by marketplace. For Flipkart, display Average Retail Price, Average Discounted Price, Discount Eligible Products, Products With Positive Discount and Average Discount Percentage. |
| Flipkart vs Myntra | Marketplace matrix with Product Count, Price Coverage, Median Recorded Price and Rating Coverage. Show recorded price field and category basis context. Comparisons describe the two dataset assortments. |
| Product and Rating Analytics | Product table by marketplace and source ID with brand, category and rating. Include Rated Products, Rating Coverage, Average Product Rating, Average Overall Rating and Reported Rating Count alongside Products With Rating Count. Use missing-metadata cards. |

Display a historical dataset note on all four pages. Flipkart values were crawled in 2015–2016. Myntra observation dates, current prices, availability and currency are unverified. The data supports listing-level distributions. It contains no transaction, traffic, customer or inventory facts, no verified listing identity across marketplaces, and no repeated observations suitable for price trends.
